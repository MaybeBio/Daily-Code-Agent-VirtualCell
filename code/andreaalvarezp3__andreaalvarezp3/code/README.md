# Hi, I'm Andrea Álvarez Pérez 👋

PhD researcher in AI for biomedicine at the [Universidad Politécnica de Madrid](https://www.upm.es) (MEDAL lab, Centre for Biomedical Technology). I work on **network medicine, foundation models and knowledge graphs for drug repurposing**, mostly in neurological diseases.

- 🧬 Single-cell RNA-seq, network medicine and the scGPT foundation model to find cell-type-specific alterations in Alzheimer's disease
- 🕸️ Building and validating biomedical knowledge graphs, and training knowledge graph embedding models in PyTorch (PyKEEN)
- 💊 Computational drug repurposing and drug combinations
- 🌍 Research stays at the University of Hamburg (CoSyBio) and TIB-Leibniz in Hannover

## Current work

🔬 **Visiting researcher at TIB – Leibniz Information Centre for Science and Technology, Hannover** (Sept–Nov 2026, MSCA THIRDWAVE project)

Completing and validating SEM-DISNET, a large biomedical knowledge graph of diseases, drugs and molecular data, as a foundation for LLM-based drug repurposing. The work combines rule mining with knowledge graph embeddings (PyKEEN, PyTorch) to predict new drug–disease relations. *Code will be released with the publication.*
