### Know-How Documents and Protocols
Markdown-based know-how documents are managed through the [`KnowHowLoader`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/loader.py#L8) class, primarily implemented in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/loader.py`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/loader.py), which is exposed via [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/__init__.py`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/__init__.py). This system parses Markdown files to extract content, titles, descriptions, and structured metadata, making them programmatically accessible to agents. Key documents include guides for sgRNA design and single-cell RNA-seq cell type annotation. The sgRNA design guide, located at [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/sgRNA_design_guide.md`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/sgRNA_design_guide.md), provides a three-tiered approach for obtaining sgRNA sequences, prioritizing validated sequences, then pre-computed datasets from sources like CRISPick, and finally de novo design guidelines. It details methods for searching curated databases, performing advanced web searches, and utilizing data from external resources. The single-cell RNA-seq cell type annotation guide, found at [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/single_cell_annotation.md`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/single_cell_annotation.md), outlines best practices for annotating cell types in single-cell RNA-seq data. It covers manual marker-based annotation, automated annotation using pre-trained classifiers, and reference-based label transfer, offering a recommended workflow, practical tips, common pitfalls, and a tool selection guide. Supporting resources, such as CRISPR experiment data downloads from [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/resource/CRISPick_download_links.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/know_how/resource/CRISPick_download_links.txt), are also integrated into this system.

An extensive collection of laboratory protocols is also available, primarily housed within [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols). These protocols cover a broad spectrum of molecular biology, cell biology, and general laboratory techniques, sourced largely from Addgene and Thermo Fisher Scientific. These detailed instructions guide users through experimental procedures, sample preparation, and analytical methods. For instance, the [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene) directory contains protocols for viral vector production (AAV and lentivirus), including detailed steps for AAV production ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Production.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Production.txt)), purification ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Purification.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Purification.txt)), and titration using qPCR ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Titration by qPCR.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV Titration by qPCR.txt)) or ddPCR ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV ddPCR Titration.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ AAV ddPCR Titration.txt)). It also includes protocols for nucleic acid manipulation, such as general mammalian cell transfection ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ General Transfection.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ General Transfection.txt)), various cloning techniques (e.g., Gibson Assembly in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Gibson Assembly Protocol.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Gibson Assembly Protocol.txt) and Ligation Independent Cloning in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Ligation Independent Cloning.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Ligation Independent Cloning.txt)), and DNA/RNA purification. Furthermore, protocols for antibody purification and validation (e.g., indirect ELISA in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Antibody Validation Using the Indirect ELISA Method.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Antibody Validation Using the Indirect ELISA Method.txt) and affinity purification in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Affinity Purification of Recombinant Antibodies with Protein A or Protein G.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/addgene/Addgene_ Affinity Purification of Recombinant Antibodies with Protein A or Protein G.txt)) and cell line isolation are provided.

The [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher) subdirectory focuses on cell biology techniques, encompassing cell culture, proliferation, viability, apoptosis, and microscopy sample preparation. This includes guidelines for adherent and suspension cell culture, cell freezing, various cell dissociation methods, and a range of assays for cell proliferation (e.g., BrdU labeling in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/BrdU Labeling & Detection Protocol _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/BrdU Labeling & Detection Protocol _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)), viability (e.g., Trypan Blue Exclusion in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Trypan Blue Exclusion _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Trypan Blue Exclusion _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)), and apoptosis (e.g., Annexin V staining in [`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/BestProtocols_ Annexin V Staining Protocol for Flow Cytometry _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/BestProtocols_ Annexin V Staining Protocol for Flow Cytometry _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)). Immunostaining protocols for targets like actin ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Actin Staining Protocol _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Actin Staining Protocol _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)), nuclear staining ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/DAPI Protocol for Fluorescence Imaging _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/DAPI Protocol for Fluorescence Imaging _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)), and specific instructions for staining 3D cell cultures ([`/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Staining 3D Cell Cultures for Imaging _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt`](https://github.com/snap-stanford/biomni/biomni/tool/protocols/thermofisher/Staining 3D Cell Cultures for Imaging _ Thermo Fisher Scientific - US.txt)) are also available, alongside ELISA and protein quantification methods.
