### Addressing Technical Batch Effects with Continual Pretraining
The data-driven, self-supervised pre-training approach of [`scGPT`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/README.md#L1) aims to reconstruct gene expression, which inherently avoids human bias in feature selection. However, this method does not explicitly mitigate technical variations, commonly referred to as batch effects. These batch effects can be categorized into two types: type I factors, which are global effects such as differences in cell capturing times and may be partially mitigated by pre-training, and type II factors, which are gene-specific influences like sequencing protocols. Type II factors can alter gene expression distributions and are often treated as important signals rather than noise by the pre-training process.

To address the challenges posed by technical batch effects and enhance zero-shot performance, especially when datasets exhibit observable technical variations, the [`scGPT_CP`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot/README.md#L18) model was developed. This model is a continually pretrained version of the [`scGPT`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/README.md#L1) whole-human model checkpoint, further supervised with cell type labels from the [`Tabula Sapiens`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/data/cellxgene/metainfo.json#L52) dataset during its continual pre-training phase. By incorporating cell type information, [`scGPT_CP`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot/README.md#L18) intrinsically considers both cell types and technical batch effects, leading to improved integration and a more unbiased data-driven pre-training outcome. The zero-shot capabilities, including reference mapping and data integration, are further detailed in [Zero-Shot Applications and Tutorials](#zero-shot-applications-and-tutorials) and demonstrated through tutorials within the [`/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot) directory. The documentation in [`/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot/README.md`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot/README.md) provides further discussion on these challenges and how [`scGPT_CP`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/tutorials/zero-shot/README.md#L18) offers a solution. This approach maintains flexibility for fine-tuning while providing a robust foundation for various analytical objectives.
