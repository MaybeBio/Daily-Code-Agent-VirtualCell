### Generative Models and Perturbation Prediction
The [`TransformerGenerator`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L27) model, primarily defined in [`/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py), is designed for generative tasks within single-cell gene expression data, focusing on masked gene modeling and the prediction of perturbation effects. This model extends the core transformer architecture to address tasks beyond simple classification or embedding generation.

The [`TransformerGenerator`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L27) integrates a [`GeneEncoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/model.py#L717) for gene embeddings, a [`ContinuousValueEncoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/model.py#L759) for expression values, and a unique [`pert_encoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L80) for handling perturbation flags. These components feed into a transformer encoder, similar to other models within the scGPT framework, but the distinguishing features lie in its decoders and specialized methods.

For predicting gene expression values, the model employs an [`AffineExprDecoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L373), which is specifically designed to output gene expression values and can optionally model explicit zero probabilities, a common characteristic of single-cell RNA sequencing data. This decoder can also predict perturbation effects, allowing the model to simulate how gene expression might change under different experimental conditions. The [`pred_perturb`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L292) method within [`TransformerGenerator`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L27) is dedicated to this task, enabling dynamic selection of genes and mapping them to vocabulary IDs for perturbation prediction.

Beyond expression prediction, the [`TransformerGenerator`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/generation_model.py#L27) also supports cell-type classification via a [`ClsDecoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/model.py#L884) and masked value prediction with an [`MVCDecoder`](https://github.com/bowang-lab/scgpt/scgpt/model/model.py#L915), demonstrating its multi-task capabilities within a generative framework. The model's design emphasizes modularity, allowing for flexible configuration of encoders and decoders, and integrates fast transformer backends for computational efficiency, particularly when handling large datasets.
